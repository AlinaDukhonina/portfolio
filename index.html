Game (C++)

In spring and summer, I took a C++ programming course. During the course, I completed a list of projects, and my favorite one is the game "Kill It All".
The idea for the project came to me after playing "Champion Island" by Google. It includes one mini-game that is really hard to master, so I liked the idea of creating something similar, but easier and shorter! The development process was really interesting and helpful for my C++ knowledge. I really enjoyed coding! I drew all the game objects myself to make them look the way I wanted while keeping the style of the original game. There are no enemies, so it is great for practicing accuracy!

Game (Web)

My first web game ever. For a long time, I wanted to create my own game, but I had no idea what it could be! I thought about it a lot, and one day I came up with the idea of making a quest game with hints and a short story. I wanted it to be completely made by me, so I drew all the items myself. It was quite hard to develop the concept and design the locations, but I really enjoyed working on this game! It is not too difficult, yet very engaging, and I was able to add everything I wanted!

Festival volunteering

Festivals are always full of interesting people with unique ideas, especially multifandom festivals filled with artistic people. At ToFu Fest, I met a lot of people and helped them during the event alongside other free-zone volunteers. We worked together to make the festival fun and unforgettable for visitors. There were many energetic kids, which required us to watch over the area more carefully, but they were also very creative, making the festival lively and diverse! At AFC ZONE, I worked with the same team in the free zone, though the atmosphere was completely different due to a different audience. There were lectures and quizzes, and I even got to participate in one during my break. At the same time, both festivals involve a lot of work before and after the event to prepare and clean up the venue. It is a great experience, and I hope to continue volunteering at festivals to create the best possible experience for visitors.
